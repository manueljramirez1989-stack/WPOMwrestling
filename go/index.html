    What the score means

    Every result at Super 32, Fargo and NHSCA is scored by how far a wrestler advanced and where he finished, plus wins over ranked opponents. State titles add a bonus.

    College coaches search the board by weight, class and score. Claiming your profile lets you check your results and add what is missing.

    Coaches: build your board
